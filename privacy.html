Gemlings

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Gemlings is an Android game published by Markus. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. It describes the version of Gemlings currently available on Google Play.

The short version. Gemlings does not collect, store, or share your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours. The only thing that leaves your phone is whatever Google's ad service needs to show you an advertisement.

What stays on your device

Your progress — the creatures you have met, the places you have found, the ground you have walked, your settings, and your daily streak — is saved only in the app's own storage on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. Nobody, including Markus, can see it.

What is not collected

Gemlings does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or any identifier that could be used to recognise you. Markus runs no analytics and no crash reporting, and receives nothing about you from anyone. The only third party involved is Google, and only in the two situations described below.

Advertising

Gemlings shows two kinds of advertisement. You can choose to watch a short video in exchange for a reward, and a full-screen advertisement may appear at a natural break in play, such as when you arrive back at camp or move between dig sites. An advertisement never appears in the middle of an action, and there are no banners.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When one is shown, Google receives your device's advertising ID, general device information, and approximate location derived from your IP address, and uses them to select and measure the ad. Markus does not receive this information and cannot identify you from it.

  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android under Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before the first ad, and you can change your answer later under Privacy choices in the game's settings.

A one-time in-app purchase permanently removes advertising from the game. After that purchase no ad is ever requested and nothing is sent to the ad service.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Markus never sees your payment details. Google confirms only whether the purchase exists, which is what restores it if you reinstall the game or change phones.

Permissions

The app requests only what it needs to run: network access and network state, vibration for strike feedback, audio settings for sound, a wake lock so the screen does not sleep during play, the advertising ID used by the ad service, and Google Play billing for the one-time purchase. It does not request the microphone, storage, location, or contacts.

Children

Gemlings is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Advertising in the game is served by Google AdMob, which has its own age-appropriate requirements. If you believe a child has used the app and you want the advertising ID cleared, reset it in Android settings as described above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Gemlings adds a feature that changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
