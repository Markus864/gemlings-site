Gemlings

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Gemlings is an Android game published by Markus. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. It describes the version of Gemlings currently available on Google Play.

The short version. Gemlings does not collect, store, or share your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. The game works with no internet connection.

What stays on your device

Your progress — the creatures you have met, the places you have found, the ground you have walked, your settings, and your daily streak — is saved only in the app's own storage on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. Nobody, including Markus, can see it.

What is not collected

Gemlings does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or any identifier that could be used to recognise you. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking or advertising.

Permissions

The app requests only what it needs to run: network access and network state (used by the Android framework; the game itself makes no network requests), vibration for strike feedback, audio settings for sound, and a wake lock so the screen does not sleep during play. It does not request the microphone, storage, location, or contacts.

Children

Gemlings is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Gemlings adds a feature that changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
